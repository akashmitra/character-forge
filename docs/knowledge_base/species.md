# Chapter 4: Species Descriptions

Ten species, alphabetical order: Aasimar, Dragonborn, Dwarf, Elf, Gnome, Goliath, Halfling, Human, Orc, Tiefling.

---

## Aasimar

Mortals carrying a spark of the Upper Planes — descended from an angelic being or infused with celestial power, able to channel light, healing, and heavenly fury. Can arise among any mortal population; resemble their parents but live up to 160 years, with celestial markers (metallic freckles, luminous eyes, a halo, angelic skin tones) that grow more pronounced as the aasimar learns to reveal their nature.

**Traits**
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium or Small (choose)
- **Speed:** 30 ft

- **Celestial Resistance:** Resistance to Necrotic and Radiant damage.
- **Darkvision:** 60 ft.
- **Healing Hands:** Magic action; touch a creature and roll a number of d4s = Proficiency Bonus; target regains that many HP. 1/Long Rest.
- **Light Bearer:** Knows the *Light* cantrip (Charisma-based).
- **Celestial Revelation** (level 3+): Bonus Action transformation, lasts 1 minute or until ended, 1/Long Rest. Once per turn during it, deal extra damage (= Proficiency Bonus; Necrotic for Necrotic Shroud, Radiant for the other two) on a hit with an attack or spell. Choose one option each time:
  - **Heavenly Wings:** spectral wings, Fly Speed = Speed.
  - **Inner Radiance:** Bright Light 10 ft/Dim Light +10 ft; at end of each of your turns, creatures within 10 ft take Radiant damage = Proficiency Bonus.
  - **Necrotic Shroud:** non-ally creatures within 10 ft must succeed a Charisma save (DC 8 + Cha mod + Prof Bonus) or be Frightened until end of your next turn.

## Dragonborn

Descended from chromatic/metallic dragon eggs — origin myths credit either the dragon gods Bahamut and Tiamat, or dragons acting alone. Wingless, bipedal, scaly, horned, resembling their draconic ancestry in coloration.

**Traits**
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium (5–7 ft)
- **Speed:** 30 ft

- **Draconic Ancestry:** choose a dragon type (below), determining Breath Weapon/Resistance damage type.

| Dragon | Damage Type | Dragon | Damage Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Black | Acid | Gold | Fire |
| Blue | Lightning | Green | Poison |
| Brass | Fire | Red | Fire |
| Bronze | Lightning | Silver | Cold |
| Copper | Acid | White | Cold |

- **Breath Weapon:** replaces one Attack action attack; 15-ft Cone or 30-ft Line (5 ft wide), choose shape each use. Targets make Dex save (DC 8 + Con mod + Prof Bonus); 1d10 damage of ancestry type on fail (half on success). Damage scales: 2d10 at level 5, 3d10 at level 11, 4d10 at level 17. Uses = Prof Bonus per Long Rest.
- **Damage Resistance:** to ancestry damage type.
- **Darkvision:** 60 ft.
- **Draconic Flight** (level 5+): Bonus Action, spectral wings, Fly Speed = Speed, lasts 10 min or until retracted/Incapacitated. 1/Long Rest.

## Dwarf

Raised from the earth by a forge deity (Moradin, Reorx, or other names) and gifted affinity for stone, metal, and underground living; resilient, ~350-year lifespan. Squat, often bearded; ancient legends of conflict with giants and Underdark monsters. Some settlements identify as hill dwarves or mountain dwarves (per-world, e.g. Greyhawk, Dragonlance).

**Traits**
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium (4–5 ft)
- **Speed:** 30 ft

- **Darkvision:** 120 ft.
- **Dwarven Resilience:** Resistance to Poison damage; Advantage on saves to avoid/end Poisoned.
- **Dwarven Toughness:** HP max +1, and +1 again each level gained.
- **Stonecunning:** Bonus Action; Tremorsense 60 ft for 10 minutes while on/touching stone (natural or worked). Uses = Prof Bonus per Long Rest.

## Elf

Created by the god Corellon; original elves could shape-shift at will until Corellon cursed them for conspiring with Lolth (who tried to usurp him). After Lolth's banishment to the Abyss, most elves were forgiven but never regained shape-shifting; they withdrew to the Feywild, later exploring other planes including the Material Plane. Pointed ears, no facial/body hair, ~750-year lifespan; don't sleep, instead entering a meditative trance while remaining aware of surroundings. Long habitation (1,000+ years) in an environment subtly transforms elves, producing subraces: drow, high elves, wood elves.

- **Drow:** shaped by the Underdark (though some avoid it while keeping its magic); in Eberron, dwell in rainforests/ruins of Xen'drik.
- **High Elves:** infused with Feywild/Material Plane crossing-magic; called sun/moon elves (Forgotten Realms), Silvanesti/Qualinesti (Dragonlance), Aereni (Eberron).
- **Wood Elves:** carry primeval forest magic; also called wild/green/forest elves; Grugach (Greyhawk), Kagonesti (Dragonlance), Tairnadal (Eberron).

**Traits**
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium (5–6 ft)
- **Speed:** 30 ft

- **Darkvision:** 60 ft.
- **Elven Lineage:** choose a lineage (below) for its level 1 benefit; gain a higher-level spell at character levels 3 and 5 (always prepared, castable once free per Long Rest, or with spell slots). Spellcasting ability: Int, Wis, or Cha (chosen at selection).
- **Fey Ancestry:** Advantage on saves to avoid/end Charmed.
- **Keen Senses:** proficiency in Insight, Perception, or Survival.
- **Trance:** no need to sleep; magic can't sleep you; Long Rest completable in 4 hours of trance (retaining awareness).

**Elven Lineages**

| Lineage | Level 1 | Level 3 | Level 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drow | Darkvision range → 120 ft; know *Dancing Lights* cantrip | *Faerie Fire* | *Darkness* |
| High Elf | Know *Prestidigitation* cantrip; swap it for a different Wizard cantrip each Long Rest | *Detect Magic* | *Misty Step* |
| Wood Elf | Speed → 35 ft; know *Druidcraft* cantrip | *Longstrider* | *Pass without Trace* |

## Gnome

Magical folk made by gods of invention, illusion, and underground life; historically secretive, living in forests/burrows, relying on cleverness (traps, tunnels) over size. Learned magic from disguised gods (Garl Glittergold, Baervan Wildwanderer, Baravar Cloakshadow), producing forest gnome and rock gnome lineages. Petite, big-eyed, pointed ears, ~425-year lifespan; fond of having "a roof" overhead, even a hat.

**Traits**
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Small (3–4 ft)
- **Speed:** 30 ft

- **Darkvision:** 60 ft.
- **Gnomish Cunning:** Advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws.
- **Gnomish Lineage:** choose one lineage below. Spellcasting ability: Int, Wis, or Cha (chosen at selection).
  - **Forest Gnome:** knows *Minor Illusion* cantrip; always has *Speak with Animals* prepared, castable without a slot a number of times = Prof Bonus per Long Rest (or with slots).
  - **Rock Gnome:** knows *Mending* and *Prestidigitation* cantrips. Can spend 10 minutes casting *Prestidigitation* to build a Tiny clockwork device (AC 5, 1 HP) — a toy, fire starter, music box, etc. — that reproduces one chosen *Prestidigitation* effect (with one sub-option locked in) when activated by touch as a Bonus Action. Up to 3 devices at once; each falls apart after 8 hours or can be dismantled by touch as a Utilize action.

## Goliath

Distant descendants of giants, bearing supernatural boons from their giant ancestry, including temporary growth toward giant size. Physical traits echo their specific giant lineage (e.g., resembling stone giants or fire giants); goliaths have carved their own path apart from giantkind's historical conflicts.

**Traits**
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium (7–8 ft)
- **Speed:** 35 ft

- **Giant Ancestry:** choose one boon below; uses = Prof Bonus per Long Rest.
  - **Cloud's Jaunt (Cloud Giant):** Bonus Action teleport up to 30 ft to a seen unoccupied space.
  - **Fire's Burn (Fire Giant):** on a hit, add 1d10 Fire damage.
  - **Frost's Chill (Frost Giant):** on a hit, add 1d6 Cold damage and reduce target's Speed by 10 ft until start of your next turn.
  - **Hill's Tumble (Hill Giant):** on a hit vs. Large or smaller, give target the Prone condition.
  - **Stone's Endurance (Stone Giant):** Reaction when taking damage; roll 1d12 + Con mod, reduce damage by that total.
  - **Storm's Thunder (Storm Giant):** Reaction when damaged by a creature within 60 ft; deal 1d8 Thunder damage back to it.
- **Large Form** (level 5+): Bonus Action, become Large size (if space allows) for 10 minutes or until ended; Advantage on Strength checks, Speed +10 ft. 1/Long Rest.
- **Powerful Build:** Advantage on saves to end Grappled; count as one size larger for carrying capacity.

## Halfling

Guided by gods of life, home, and hearth, gravitating to bucolic communities, but often adventurous. Human-child-sized, letting them move unnoticed through crowds and tight spaces. Known for "halfling luck" — an unseen protective force many attribute to gods like Yondalla, Brandobaris, and Charmalaine; ~150-year lifespan. Communities vary widely, from peaceful shires to crime syndicates (e.g., the Boromar Clan in Eberron) or territorial groups (Dark Sun). Subgroup names: strongheart halflings/stouts (underground-dwelling), lightfoot halflings/tallfellows (nomadic or living among tall folk).

**Traits**
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Small (2–3 ft)
- **Speed:** 30 ft

- **Brave:** Advantage on saves to avoid/end Frightened.
- **Halfling Nimbleness:** can move through the space of a creature one size larger (can't stop there).
- **Luck:** rerolling a natural 1 on a d20 Test (must use the new roll).
- **Naturally Stealthy:** can Hide even when obscured only by a creature at least one size larger.

## Human

Found throughout the multiverse, diverse in appearance and belief; known for ambition and resourcefulness. Scholars trace an early gathering to Sigil, birthplace of the Common language, from which humans may have spread across the multiverse.

**Traits**
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium or Small (choose)
- **Speed:** 30 ft

- **Resourceful:** gain Heroic Inspiration whenever you finish a Long Rest.
- **Skillful:** proficiency in one skill of your choice.
- **Versatile:** gain an Origin feat of your choice (Skilled recommended).

---

## Orc

Trace creation to Gruumsh, who equipped them with endurance, determination, and darkvision to survive plains, caverns, and seas. Tall, broad, gray-skinned, pointed ears, prominent lower tusks. Attitudes toward ancestral legend and Gruumsh's favor vary by individual/culture.

**Traits**
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium (6–7 ft)
- **Speed:** 30 ft

- **Adrenaline Rush:** Dash as a Bonus Action; gain Temporary HP = Prof Bonus. Uses = Prof Bonus per Short/Long Rest.
- **Darkvision:** 120 ft.
- **Relentless Endurance:** when reduced to 0 HP (not killed outright), drop to 1 HP instead. 1/Long Rest.

---

## Tiefling

Born in the Lower Planes or descended from fiendish ancestors (devil, demon, or other Fiend). The fiendish legacy grants power but doesn't dictate moral outlook — tieflings choose whether to embrace or reject it. Three legacies:

- **Abyssal:** tied to the Abyss, Pandemonium, Carceri; demonic blood; horns, fur, tusks, unusual scents common.
- **Chthonic:** tied to Carceri, Gehenna, Hades; may look cadaverous or possess succubus-like beauty, or resemble a night hag/yugoloth/other Neutral Evil fiend.
- **Infernal:** tied to Gehenna, the Nine Hells, Acheron; horns, spines, tails, golden eyes, faint sulfur/smoke smell; usually devil-descended.

**Traits**
- **Creature Type:** Humanoid
- **Size:** Medium or Small (choose)
- **Speed:** 30 ft

- **Darkvision:** 60 ft.
- **Fiendish Legacy:** choose a legacy below for its level 1 benefit; gain a higher-level spell at character levels 3 and 5 (always prepared, castable once free per Long Rest, or with spell slots). Spellcasting ability: Int, Wis, or Cha (chosen at selection).
- **Otherworldly Presence:** knows *Thaumaturgy* cantrip, using the same spellcasting ability as Fiendish Legacy.

**Fiendish Legacies**

| Legacy | Level 1 | Level 3 | Level 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Abyssal | Resistance to Poison damage; know *Poison Spray* cantrip | *Ray of Sickness* | *Hold Person* |
| Chthonic | Resistance to Necrotic damage; know *Chill Touch* cantrip | *False Life* | *Ray of Enfeeblement* |
| Infernal | Resistance to Fire damage; know *Fire Bolt* cantrip | *Hellish Rebuke* | *Darkness* |
