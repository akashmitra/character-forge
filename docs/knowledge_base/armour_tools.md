# Chapter 6: Equipment — Armor & Tools

## Armor

The Armor table lists cost, weight, and the following details for each type:

- **Category:** Light, Medium, or Heavy — determines how long it takes to don/doff.
- **Armor Class (AC):** base AC while wearing that armor (e.g., Leather Armor = 11 + Dex modifier; Chain Mail = 16 flat).
- **Strength:** if listed, the wearer's Speed is reduced by 10 ft unless their Strength meets or exceeds that score.
- **Stealth:** "Disadvantage" means the wearer has Disadvantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks.

### Armor Training
Anyone can don armor or hold a Shield, but only those trained can use them effectively. Training comes from class features or other sources; monsters are trained with any armor in their stat block.

**Light, Medium, or Heavy Armor:** without training in the category worn, you have Disadvantage on any D20 Test involving Strength or Dexterity, and you can't cast spells.

**Shield:** you only gain its AC bonus if trained with shields.

**One at a Time:** a creature can wear only one suit of armor and wield only one Shield at a time.

### Variant: Equipment Sizes
By default, you can use/wear any found equipment within reason (an orc won't fit a halfling's leather armor; a cloud giant's robe would swallow a gnome). A DM can add more realism: armor/clothing made for one specific wearer might not fit another without alteration, requiring a visit to a smith, tailor, or leatherworker at a cost of 1d4 × 10% of the item's market price.

## Tools

A tool supports specialized ability checks, crafting, or both. Each tool entry lists:

- **Ability:** the ability score used for checks with the tool.
- **Utilize:** actions available via the Utilize action, each with a DC.
- **Craft:** items craftable with the tool (see "Crafting Equipment" rules, not included here).
- **Variants:** listed separately if the tool has them (each requiring its own proficiency).

**Tool Proficiency:** proficiency adds your Proficiency Bonus to ability checks using the tool; if you're also proficient in a relevant skill, you get Advantage on that check too. Monsters are proficient with any tool in their stat block.
