# Chapter 4: Character Origins

## Overview

A character's origin has two components: **background** and **species**. Together they suggest early life and family history before the character took up adventuring (class capabilities are covered separately in Chapter 3).

## Origin Components

### Character Backgrounds
A background represents the place and occupation most formative to the character before adventuring. Each background includes:

- **Ability Scores:** lists three abilities — increase one by +2 and another by +1, or increase all three by +1. No score can exceed 20.
- **Feat:** grants a specified Origin feat (Chapter 5).
- **Skill Proficiencies:** two specified skills.
- **Tool Proficiency:** one tool — either a specific tool or a choice from a tool category (e.g., Artisan's Tools, Gaming Set, Musical Instrument).
- **Equipment:** choice between a starting equipment package (Option A) or 50 GP (Option B).

### Character Species
Species determines whether a character is human or a fantastical species (dragonborn, gnome, etc.). Species peoples originate from different worlds/planes but have spread across the multiverse. Most species live ~80 years (exceptions noted per-species); all reach physical maturity around the same age regardless of lifespan.

**Parts of a species:**
- **Creature Type:** all species in this chapter are Humanoid.
- **Size:** determined by species; some species let you choose Small or Medium.
- **Speed:** determined by species.
- **Special Traits:** unique physiological/magical characteristics; some involve a choice among options.
