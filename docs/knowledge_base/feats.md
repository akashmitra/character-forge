# Chapter 5: Feats

Feats are special features not tied to a class — representing talent, training, or expertise beyond what a class provides. Backgrounds grant a feat; classes grant Ability Score Improvement (or a qualifying alternative) at certain levels. A feat can only be taken once unless its description says otherwise.

## Parts of a Feat

- **Category:** Origin, General, Fighting Style, or Epic Boon. If instructed to pick from a specific category, the feat must belong to it.
- **Prerequisite:** must be met to take the feat (unless another feature waives it). A class prerequisite requires at least 1 level in that class.
- **Benefit:** what you gain by having the feat.
- **Repeatable:** only listed feats can be taken more than once.

## Origin Feats

**Alert** — Add Proficiency Bonus to Initiative rolls; immediately after rolling Initiative, can swap it with a willing ally's (neither can be Incapacitated).

**Crafter** — Proficiency with 3 Artisan's Tools of choice from the Fast Crafting table; 20% discount on nonmagical item purchases; after a Long Rest, can craft one Fast Crafting item (needs the associated tool proficiency) that lasts until your next Long Rest.

*Fast Crafting table:*

| Artisan's Tools | Crafted Gear |
|---|---|
| Carpenter's Tools | Ladder, Torch |
| Leatherworker's Tools | Case, Pouch |
| Mason's Tools | Block and Tackle |
| Potter's Tools | Jug, Lamp |
| Smith's Tools | Ball Bearings, Bucket, Caltrops, Grappling Hook, Iron Pot |
| Tinker's Tools | Bell, Shovel, Tinder Box |
| Weaver's Tools | Basket, Rope, Net, Tent |
| Woodcarver's Tools | Club, Greatclub, Quarterstaff |

**Healer** — *Battle Medic:* Utilize action with a Healer's Kit (1 use); target expends a Hit Die, you roll it, target heals that roll + your Proficiency Bonus. *Healing Rerolls:* reroll a natural 1 on any healing die (spell or Battle Medic), must use new roll.

**Lucky** — Luck Points = Proficiency Bonus, regained on Long Rest. Spend 1 to gain Advantage on your own d20 Test, or to impose Disadvantage on an attack roll against you.

**Magic Initiate** — Learn 2 cantrips from Cleric, Druid, or Wizard list (spellcasting ability: Int/Wis/Cha, chosen at selection); also learn 1 level 1 spell from the same list, always prepared, castable once free per Long Rest (or via spell slots). On level-up, can swap one chosen spell for another of the same level from that list. Repeatable — must choose a different spell list each time.

**Musician** — Proficiency with 3 Musical Instruments. After a Short or Long Rest, can play a song to grant Heroic Inspiration to a number of allies (who hear it) equal to Proficiency Bonus.

**Savage Attacker** — Once per turn on a weapon hit, roll the weapon's damage dice twice and use either result.

**Skilled** — Proficiency in any combination of 3 skills/tools. Repeatable.

**Tavern Brawler** — *Enhanced Unarmed Strike:* deal 1d4 + Strength mod Bludgeoning instead of normal. *Damage Rerolls:* reroll a natural 1 on Unarmed Strike damage. *Improvised Weaponry:* proficiency with improvised weapons. *Push:* once per turn, an Unarmed Strike hit (as part of Attack action) also pushes the target 5 ft.

**Tough** — HP max increases by 2× character level when taken; +2 HP max per level gained thereafter.

## General Feats

*All require Level 4+ unless noted; most also raise an ability score by 1 (max 20) as part of their benefits.*

**Ability Score Improvement** — Increase one ability score by 2, or two ability scores by 1 each (max 20 each). Repeatable.

**Actor** *(Cha 13+)* — +1 Charisma. Advantage on Charisma (Deception/Performance) to impersonate a specific person while disguised as them. Can mimic creature sounds/speech; a listener needs a Wisdom (Insight) check (DC 8 + Cha mod + Prof Bonus) to detect the fake.

**Athlete** *(Str or Dex 13+)* — +1 Str or Dex. Climb Speed = Speed. Stand from Prone using only 5 ft of movement. Running Long/High Jump after moving only 5 ft.

**Charger** *(Str or Dex 13+)* — +1 Str or Dex. Dash gains +10 ft Speed for that action. After moving 10+ ft straight toward a target then hitting with a melee attack: +1d8 damage, or push target up to 10 ft (if no more than one size larger). Once per turn.

**Chef** — +1 Con or Wis. Proficiency with Cook's Utensils. *Replenishing Meal:* during a Short Rest, cook food for 4 + Proficiency Bonus creatures; eating it and spending a Hit Die heals +1d8 extra HP. *Bolstering Treats:* with 1 hour or after a Long Rest, cook treats (= Prof Bonus) that last 8 hours; eating one (Bonus Action) grants Temporary HP = Prof Bonus.

**Crossbow Expert** *(Dex 13+)* — +1 Dex. Ignore Loading property on Hand/Heavy/Light Crossbows. No Disadvantage firing crossbows within 5 ft of an enemy. Can add ability modifier to the Light property's extra-attack damage when using a Light crossbow (if not already added).

**Crusher** — +1 Str or Con. Once per turn, a Bludgeoning-damage hit can push the target 5 ft to an unoccupied space (if no more than one size larger). Critical Hit dealing Bludgeoning damage grants Advantage on attacks against that creature until start of your next turn.

**Defensive Duelist** *(Dex 13+)* — +1 Dex. While holding a Finesse weapon, Reaction to add Proficiency Bonus to AC against one melee hit (possibly causing a miss); bonus lasts until start of next turn.

**Dual Wielder** *(Str or Dex 13+)* — +1 Str or Dex. When attacking with a Light weapon, can make an extra Bonus Action attack with a different Melee (non-Two-Handed) weapon — no ability mod to that damage unless negative. Can draw/stow two non-Two-Handed weapons at once.

**Durable** — +1 Con. Advantage on Death Saving Throws. Bonus Action: expend a Hit Die, roll it, regain that many HP.

**Elemental Adept** *(Spellcasting or Pact Magic)* — +1 Int/Wis/Cha. Choose a damage type (Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder): your spells of that type ignore Resistance, and 1s on damage dice count as 2s. Repeatable with a different type each time.

**Fey-Touched** — +1 Int/Wis/Cha. Choose one level 1 Divination or Enchantment spell; always have it and *Misty Step* prepared, each castable free once per Long Rest (plus via spell slots). Spellcasting ability = the one increased.

**Grappler** *(Str or Dex 13+)* — +1 Str or Dex. An Unarmed Strike hit can apply both Damage and Grapple (once/turn). Advantage on attacks vs. creatures you've Grappled. Speed isn't halved moving a Grappled creature your size or smaller.

**Great Weapon Master** *(Str 13+)* — +1 Str. Hitting with a Heavy weapon deals bonus damage = Proficiency Bonus. After a Critical Hit or reducing a creature to 0 HP with a Melee weapon, make a Bonus Action attack with the same weapon.

**Heavily Armored** *(Medium Armor Training)* — +1 Con or Str. Gain Heavy armor training.

**Heavy Armor Master** *(Heavy Armor Training)* — +1 Con or Str. While in Heavy armor, reduce Bludgeoning/Piercing/Slashing damage taken by an amount = Proficiency Bonus.

**Inspiring Leader** *(Wis or Cha 13+)* — +1 Wis or Cha. After a Short/Long Rest, give a performance to up to 6 allies within 30 ft (including self); each gains Temporary HP = character level + the increased ability's modifier.

**Keen Mind** *(Int 13+)* — +1 Int. Choose Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature, or Religion — gain proficiency (or Expertise if already proficient). Can take the Study action as a Bonus Action.

**Lightly Armored** — +1 Str or Dex. Gain Light armor and Shield training.

**Mage Slayer** — +1 Str or Dex. Damaging a concentrating creature gives it Disadvantage on its Concentration save. Once per Short/Long Rest, turn a failed Int/Wis/Cha save into a success.

**Martial Weapon Training** — +1 Str or Dex. Proficiency with Martial weapons.

**Medium Armor Master** *(Medium Armor Training)* — +1 Str or Dex. In Medium armor, add +3 (instead of +2) to AC from Dexterity if Dex ≥16.

**Moderately Armored** *(Light Armor Training)* — +1 Str or Dex. Gain Medium armor training.

**Mounted Combatant** — +1 Str/Dex/Wis. While mounted: Advantage on attacks vs. unmounted creatures within 5 ft of your mount that are at least one size smaller than it. Mount can avoid all damage on a successful Dex save (half on fail) from applicable effects; you can force an attack targeting your mount to hit you instead (neither can be Incapacitated for either benefit).

**Observant** *(Int or Wis 13+)* — +1 Int or Wis. Choose Insight, Investigation, or Perception — gain proficiency (or Expertise if already proficient). Can take the Search action as a Bonus Action.

**Piercer** — +1 Str or Dex. Once per turn on a Piercing hit, reroll one damage die (must use new roll). Critical Hit dealing Piercing damage: roll one additional extra damage die.

**Poisoner** — +1 Dex or Int. Your Poison damage rolls ignore Resistance to Poison. Proficiency with Poisoner's Kit; with 1 hour + 50 GP materials, craft poison doses = Proficiency Bonus. Bonus Action to apply a dose to a weapon/ammo; potent for 1 minute or until it lands a hit. On hit, target makes a Con save (DC 8 + increased ability mod + Prof Bonus) or takes 2d8 Poison damage and is Poisoned until end of your next turn.

**Polearm Master** *(Str or Dex 13+)* — +1 Dex or Str. After attacking with a Quarterstaff/Spear/Heavy+Reach weapon, Bonus Action melee attack with the opposite end (1d4 Bludgeoning). While holding such a weapon, Reaction to attack a creature entering your reach.

**Resilient** — Choose an ability lacking saving throw proficiency; +1 to that score (max 20) and gain saving throw proficiency with it.

**Ritual Caster** *(Int, Wis, or Cha 13+)* — +1 Int/Wis/Cha. Choose a number of level 1 Ritual-tagged spells = Proficiency Bonus; always prepared, castable with spell slots. Add another Ritual spell whenever Proficiency Bonus increases. *Quick Ritual:* cast a prepared Ritual spell at its normal casting time (not the extended Ritual time) without a spell slot, once per Long Rest.

**Savage Attacker** — *(see Origin Feats above)*

**Sentinel** *(Str or Dex 13+)* — +1 Str or Dex. Opportunity Attack triggers when a creature within 5 ft takes the Disengage action or attacks someone other than you. A hit with such an Opportunity Attack reduces the target's Speed to 0 for the rest of the turn.

**Shadow-Touched** — +1 Int/Wis/Cha. Choose one level 1 Illusion or Necromancy spell; always have it and *Invisibility* prepared, each castable free once per Long Rest (plus via spell slots). Spellcasting ability = the one increased.

**Sharpshooter** *(Dex 13+)* — +1 Dex. Ranged attacks ignore Half/Three-Quarters Cover. No Disadvantage firing Ranged weapons within 5 ft of an enemy or at long range.

**Shield Master** *(Shield Training)* — +1 Str. On a melee hit (Attack action) while a Shield is equipped, can Shield Bash: target makes a Str save (DC 8 + Str mod + Prof Bonus) or is pushed 5 ft / knocked Prone (your choice), once per turn. Holding a Shield: Reaction to take no damage on a successful Dex save that would otherwise halve damage.

**Skill Expert** — +1 any ability score. Gain proficiency in one skill of choice. Gain Expertise in one skill you're already proficient in.

**Skulker** *(Dex 13+)* — +1 Dex. Blindsight 10 ft. Advantage on Stealth checks made as part of Hide during combat. A missed attack roll while hidden doesn't reveal your location.

**Slasher** — +1 Str or Dex. Once per turn on a Slashing hit, reduce target's Speed by 10 ft until start of your next turn. Critical Hit dealing Slashing damage: target has Disadvantage on attack rolls until start of your next turn.

**Speedy** *(Dex or Con 13+)* — +1 Dex or Con. Speed +10 ft. Dash ignores Difficult Terrain cost for that turn. Opportunity Attacks against you have Disadvantage.

**Spell Sniper** *(Spellcasting or Pact Magic)* — +1 Int/Wis/Cha. Spell attacks ignore Half/Three-Quarters Cover. No Disadvantage casting attack-roll spells within 5 ft of an enemy. Spells requiring an attack roll with range ≥10 ft get +60 ft range.

**Telekinetic** — +1 Int/Wis/Cha. Learn *Mage Hand* (no Verbal/Somatic components, spectral hand can be Invisible, range +30 ft). Bonus Action: telekinetically shove a creature within 30 ft — Str save (DC 8 + ability mod + Prof Bonus) or moved 5 ft toward/away.

**Telepathic** — +1 Int/Wis/Cha. Speak telepathically (in a language you know) to any creature within 60 ft that knows that language (they can't reply telepathically). Always have *Detect Thoughts* prepared, castable free once per Long Rest (no components needed) or via spell slots.

**Tough** — *(see Origin Feats above)*

**War Caster** *(Spellcasting or Pact Magic)* — +1 Int/Wis/Cha. Advantage on Concentration saves. Reaction: cast a one-action-casting-time spell targeting only the provoking creature instead of an Opportunity Attack. Can perform Somatic components even with weapons/Shield in hand(s).

**Weapon Master** — +1 Str or Dex. Use the mastery property of one Simple or Martial weapon you're proficient with; can change the chosen weapon type after each Long Rest.

## Fighting Style Feats

*Require the Fighting Style feature.*

**Archery** — +2 to attack rolls with Ranged weapons.

**Blind Fighting** — Blindsight, range 10 ft.

**Defense** — +1 AC while wearing Light, Medium, or Heavy armor.

**Dueling** — +2 damage with a Melee weapon held in one hand (and no other weapons).

**Great Weapon Fighting** — On a Melee weapon held with two hands (Two-Handed or Versatile), treat 1s or 2s on damage dice as 3s.

**Interception** — Reaction: when a creature you see hits another within 5 ft of you, reduce the damage to the target by 1d10 + Proficiency Bonus. Requires holding a Shield or a Simple/Martial weapon.

**Protection** — Reaction: when a creature you see attacks another target within 5 ft of you, interpose your Shield to impose Disadvantage on that attack roll and all further attacks vs. that target until start of your next turn (as long as you stay within 5 ft).

**Thrown Weapon Fighting** — +2 damage on a ranged hit with a Thrown weapon.

**Two-Weapon Fighting** — Add your ability modifier to the damage of the extra Light-property attack (if not already added).

**Unarmed Fighting** — Unarmed Strike hits deal 1d6 + Strength mod Bludgeoning (1d8 if no weapon/Shield held). At the start of each turn, deal 1d4 Bludgeoning to a creature you're Grappling.

## Epic Boon Feats

*All require Level 19+; ability score increases here can raise a score to a maximum of 30.*

**Boon of Combat Prowess** — +1 any score. *Peerless Aim:* turn a missed attack roll into a hit; once per turn (recharges start of next turn).

**Boon of Dimensional Travel** — +1 any score. *Blink Steps:* after taking the Attack or Magic action, teleport up to 30 ft to a seen unoccupied space.

**Boon of Energy Resistance** — +1 any score. Resistance to 2 chosen damage types (Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Necrotic, Poison, Psychic, Radiant, Thunder), changeable each Long Rest. Reaction on taking damage of a resisted type: redirect the same amount/type toward another creature within 60 ft (not behind Total Cover) — Dex save (DC 8 + Con mod + Prof Bonus) or take 2d12 + Con mod damage.

**Boon of Fate** — +1 any score. When you or a creature within 60 ft succeeds/fails a D20 Test, roll 2d4 and apply as bonus or penalty to that roll. Recharges on rolling Initiative or finishing a Short/Long Rest.

**Boon of Fortitude** — +1 any score. HP max +40. Whenever you regain HP, also regain bonus HP = Con modifier (once per turn, until start of next turn).

**Boon of Irresistible Offense** — +1 Str or Dex. Bludgeoning/Piercing/Slashing damage you deal ignores Resistance. Rolling a natural 20 on an attack: extra damage equal to the increased ability score (same damage type as the attack).

**Boon of Recovery** — +1 any score. *Last Stand:* when you'd drop to 0 HP, drop to 1 instead and regain HP = half your max; once per Long Rest. *Recover Vitality:* pool of ten d10s; Bonus Action to expend some, roll, and heal that total; pool refills on Long Rest.

**Boon of Skill** — +1 any score. Proficiency in all skills. Gain Expertise in one skill you lack it in.

**Boon of Speed** — +1 any score. Bonus Action Disengage that also ends the Grappled condition on you. Speed +30 ft.

**Boon of Spell Recall** *(Spellcasting feature)* — +1 Int/Wis/Cha. When casting a spell with a level 1–4 slot, roll 1d4; if it matches the slot's level, the slot isn't expended.

**Boon of the Night Spirit** — +1 any score. In Dim Light/Darkness: Bonus Action to gain the Invisible condition (ends after your next action/Bonus Action/Reaction). Also, in Dim Light/Darkness, Resistance to all damage except Psychic and Radiant.

**Boon of Truesight** — +1 any score. Truesight, range 60 ft.
