# Cleric

*A Miraculous Priest of Divine Power*

Clerics draw power from the realms of the gods and harness it to work miracles. Blessed by a deity, pantheon, or other immortal entity, a Cleric reaches into the divine magic of the Outer Planes to bolster allies and battle foes. Because their power is a divine gift, Clerics typically associate with temples dedicated to the source of their magic, though harnessing that power doesn't require specific training — Clerics may simply learn helpful prayers and rites. Not every temple member is a Cleric; many priests serve through devotion alone, without magic.

## Core Cleric Traits

| Trait | Detail |
|---|---|
| Primary Ability | Wisdom |
| Hit Point Die | d8 per Cleric level |
| Saving Throw Proficiencies | Wisdom and Charisma |
| Skill Proficiencies | Choose 2: History, Insight, Medicine, Persuasion, or Religion |
| Weapon Proficiencies | Simple weapons |
| Armor Training | Light and Medium armor, and Shields |
| Starting Equipment | Choose A or B: (A) Chain Shirt, Shield, Mace, Holy Symbol, Priest's Pack, and 7 GP; or (B) 110 GP |

## Becoming a Cleric

**As a Level 1 Character:** gain all Core Cleric Traits, plus level 1 features from the Cleric Features table.

**As a Multiclass Character:** gain Hit Point Die and Light/Medium armor + Shield training from the Core Traits table, plus level 1 Cleric features.

## Class Features

### Level 1: Spellcasting
**Cantrips:** know 3 from the Cleric spell list (*Guidance*, *Sacred Flame*, *Thaumaturgy* recommended). Swap one cantrip per level-up. Learn additional cantrips at levels 4 and 10.

**Spell Slots:** per table; regain all on Long Rest.

**Prepared Spells of Level 1+:** start with 4 level 1 spells (*Bless*, *Cure Wounds*, *Guiding Bolt*, *Shield of Faith* recommended). List grows per the Prepared Spells column; must be a level you have slots for. Always-prepared spells from other features don't count against this limit but count as Cleric spells. Can freely change the whole prepared list after each Long Rest.

**Spellcasting Ability:** Wisdom. **Spellcasting Focus:** a Holy Symbol.

### Level 1: Divine Order
Choose one sacred role:
- **Protector:** proficiency with Martial weapons, training with Heavy armor.
- **Thaumaturge:** learn one extra cantrip from the Cleric spell list; bonus to Intelligence (Arcana or Religion) checks = Wisdom modifier (min. +1).

### Level 2: Channel Divinity
Channel divine energy for magical effects. Starts with two options: **Divine Spark** and **Turn Undead** (below); gain more options at higher levels. Usable twice; regain 1 use per Short Rest, all uses per Long Rest (more uses at higher Cleric levels per table). DC for any save = your spellcasting DC.

- **Divine Spark:** Magic action; point Holy Symbol at a creature within 30 ft. Roll 1d8 + Wisdom modifier — either restore that many HP to the target, or force a Constitution save (fail: that much Necrotic or Radiant damage, your choice; success: half, rounded down). Extra d8 at levels 7 (2d8), 13 (3d8), 18 (4d8).
- **Turn Undead:** Magic action; each Undead of choice within 30 ft makes a Wisdom save or gains Frightened and Incapacitated for 1 minute, trying to flee. Ends early if it takes damage, or if you're Incapacitated or die.

### Level 3: Cleric Subclass
Choose a subclass: Life Domain, Light Domain, Trickery Domain, or War Domain. Gain its features at or below your current level for the rest of your career.

### Level 4, 8, 12, 16: Ability Score Improvement
Gain the Ability Score Improvement feat or another qualifying feat.

### Level 5: Sear Undead
When using Turn Undead, roll a number of d8s = Wisdom modifier (min. 1) — each Undead that fails its save against that use takes that total as Radiant damage (doesn't end the Turn Undead effect).

### Level 7: Blessed Strikes
Choose one (locked in if chosen via an older-book subclass instead):
- **Divine Strike:** once per turn on a weapon hit, add extra 1d8 Necrotic or Radiant damage (your choice).
- **Potent Spellcasting:** add Wisdom modifier to damage dealt by Cleric cantrips.

### Level 10: Divine Intervention
Magic action: choose any Cleric spell of level 5 or lower that doesn't require a Reaction, and cast it free (no slot, no Material components). Once per Long Rest.

### Level 14: Improved Blessed Strikes
Upgrades your Level 7 choice:
- **Divine Strike:** extra damage increases to 2d8.
- **Potent Spellcasting:** dealing damage with a Cleric cantrip also grants Temporary HP (= 2× Wisdom modifier) to yourself or another creature within 60 ft.

### Level 19: Epic Boon
Gain an Epic Boon feat or another qualifying feat. Boon of Fate recommended.

### Level 20: Greater Divine Intervention
Divine Intervention can now select *Wish* as the chosen spell. Doing so locks out Divine Intervention until you finish 2d4 Long Rests.

## Cleric Subclasses

### Life Domain
*Soothe the Hurts of the World.* Focuses on positive life-sustaining energy; masters of healing, associated with agricultural, healing/endurance, and home/community deities.

- **Level 3 — Disciple of Life:** when a spell cast with a spell slot restores HP to a creature, that creature regains additional HP (2 + the slot's level) on the turn the spell is cast.
- **Level 3 — Life Domain Spells:** always-prepared spells at specified levels:

| Cleric Level | Prepared Spells |
|---|---|
| 3 | Aid, Bless, Cure Wounds, Lesser Restoration |
| 5 | Mass Healing Word, Revivify |
| 7 | Aura of Life, Death Ward |
| 9 | Greater Restoration, Mass Cure Wounds |

- **Level 3 — Preserve Life:** Magic action, expend a Channel Divinity use to restore HP totaling 5× Cleric level, divided among Bloodied creatures within 30 ft (including self); no single creature can be healed above half its max HP.
- **Level 6 — Blessed Healer:** immediately after casting a spell with a slot that heals others (not yourself), regain HP = 2 + the slot's level.
- **Level 17 — Supreme Healing:** healing dice (from spells or Channel Divinity) aren't rolled — use the maximum possible value instead.

### Light Domain
*Bring Light to Banish Darkness.* Emphasizes blazing fire and revelation; associated with gods of truth, vigilance, beauty, insight, and renewal.

- **Level 3 — Light Domain Spells:**

| Cleric Level | Prepared Spells |
|---|---|
| 3 | Burning Hands, Faerie Fire, Scorching Ray, See Invisibility |
| 5 | Daylight, Fireball |
| 7 | Arcane Eye, Wall of Fire |
| 9 | Flame Strike, Scrying |

- **Level 3 — Radiance of the Dawn:** Magic action, expend a Channel Divinity use for a flash of light in a 30-ft Emanation — dispels magical Darkness in the area; chosen creatures make a Constitution save or take 2d10 + Cleric level Radiant damage (half on success).
- **Level 3 — Warding Flare:** Reaction when a seen creature within 30 ft makes an attack roll — impose Disadvantage on it. Uses = Wisdom modifier (min. 1) per Long Rest.
- **Level 6 — Improved Warding Flare:** Warding Flare uses also recharge on Short Rest; using it also grants the attack's target Temporary HP (2d6 + Wisdom mod).
- **Level 17 — Corona of Light:** Magic action, emit sunlight (Bright Light 60 ft + Dim Light 30 ft) for 1 minute or until dismissed. Enemies in the Bright Light have Disadvantage on saves against your Radiance of the Dawn and any Fire/Radiant spell. Uses = Wisdom modifier (min. 1) per Long Rest.

### Trickery Domain
*Make Mischief and Challenge Authority.* Magic of deception, illusion, and stealth; associated with gods of mischief, change, and social upheaval, and patrons of thieves and rebels.

- **Level 3 — Blessing of the Trickster:** Magic action; grant yourself or a willing creature within 30 ft Advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks, until a Long Rest or reuse.
- **Level 3 — Invoke Duplicity:** Bonus Action, expend a Channel Divinity use to create an intangible illusory duplicate of yourself within 30 ft, lasting 1 minute (dismissible, ends on Incapacitated). While it persists:
  - **Cast Spells:** cast as though you were in the illusion's space (using your own senses).
  - **Distract:** Advantage on attacks against a creature within 5 ft of both you and the illusion.
  - **Move:** Bonus Action to move the illusion up to 30 ft within 120 ft of you.
- **Level 3 — Trickery Domain Spells:**

| Cleric Level | Prepared Spells |
|---|---|
| 3 | Charm Person, Disguise Self, Invisibility, Pass without Trace |
| 5 | Hypnotic Pattern, Nondetection |
| 7 | Confusion, Dimension Door |
| 9 | Dominate Person, Modify Memory |

- **Level 6 — Trickster's Transposition:** when using the Bonus Action to create/move the illusion, you can instead teleport, swapping places with it.
- **Level 17 — Improved Duplicity:** *Shared Distraction:* attacks by you and allies against a creature within 5 ft of the illusion have Advantage. *Healing Illusion:* when the illusion ends, you or a chosen creature within 5 ft regains HP = Cleric level.

### War Domain
*Inspire Valor and Smite Foes.* Excel in battle, treating combat prowess or acts of violence as prayer; associated with gods of honor, destruction, or neutral war deities.

- **Level 3 — Guided Strike:** when you or a creature within 30 ft misses an attack, expend a Channel Divinity use to add +10 to that roll (potentially turning it into a hit). Requires a Reaction when used on another creature's roll.
- **Level 3 — War Domain Spells:**

| Cleric Level | Prepared Spells |
|---|---|
| 3 | Guiding Bolt, Magic Weapon, Shield of Faith, Spiritual Weapon |
| 5 | Crusader's Mantle, Spirit Guardians |
| 7 | Fire Shield, Freedom of Movement |
| 9 | Hold Monster, Steel Wind Strike |

- **Level 3 — War Priest:** Bonus Action, make one weapon or Unarmed Strike attack. Uses = Wisdom modifier (min. 1) per Short/Long Rest.
- **Level 6 — War God's Blessing:** expend a Channel Divinity use to cast *Shield of Faith* or *Spiritual Weapon* free (no slot); the spell doesn't need Concentration, instead lasting 1 minute (ends early if recast, if Incapacitated, or if you die).
- **Level 17 — Avatar of Battle:** Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.
