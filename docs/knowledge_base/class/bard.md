# Bard

*An Inspiring Performer of Music, Dance, and Magic*

Bards invoke magic through music, dance, and verse — inspiring others, soothing hurts, disheartening foes, and creating illusions. Bards believe the multiverse was spoken into existence, and that remnants of its Words of Creation still resound throughout every plane; bardic magic attempts to harness those words, which transcend language. Bards are fascinated by nearly everything, becoming masters of performance, magic, and wit. Their lives are spent traveling, gathering lore, and living on audience gratitude, but their depth of knowledge and magical mastery sets them apart from ordinary entertainers.

| Starting Equipment | Choose A or B: (A) Leather Armor, 2 Daggers, Musical Instrument of choice, Entertainer's Pack, and 19 GP; or (B) 90 GP |

## Becoming a Bard

**As a Level 1 Character:** gain all Core Bard Traits, plus level 1 features from the Bard Features table.

**As a Multiclass Character:** gain Hit Point Die, proficiency in one skill of choice, proficiency with one Musical Instrument of choice, and Light armor training from the Core Traits table, plus level 1 Bard features.

## Class Features

### Level 1: Bardic Inspiration
Represented by a Bardic Inspiration die (d6). Bonus Action: inspire a creature within 60 ft that can see/hear you, giving it one die (max one die at a time). Within the next hour, on failing a D20 Test, it can roll the die and add it to the d20 (potentially turning failure into success); the die is expended when rolled.

**Number of Uses:** = Charisma modifier (min. 1); all uses regained on Long Rest.

**At Higher Levels:** die becomes d8 at level 5, d10 at level 10, d12 at level 15.

### Level 1: Spellcasting
**Cantrips:** know 2 from the Bard spell list (*Dancing Lights* and *Vicious Mockery* recommended). Swap one cantrip per level-up. Learn additional cantrips at levels 4 and 10.

**Spell Slots:** per Bard Features table; regain all on Long Rest.

**Prepared Spells of Level 1+:** start with 4 level 1 spells (*Charm Person*, *Color Spray*, *Dissonant Whispers*, *Healing Word* recommended). List grows per the Prepared Spells column; spells must be of a level you have slots for. Spells always-prepared from other features don't count against this limit but count as Bard spells. Swap one prepared spell per level-up.

**Spellcasting Ability:** Charisma. **Spellcasting Focus:** a Musical Instrument.

### Level 2: Expertise
Expertise in 2 skill proficiencies of choice (Performance and Persuasion recommended). At level 9, Expertise in 2 more.

### Level 2: Jack of All Trades
Add half your Proficiency Bonus (round down) to ability checks using a skill you lack proficiency in (and that doesn't already use your Proficiency Bonus).

### Level 3: Bard Subclass
Choose a subclass: College of Dance, College of Glamour, College of Lore, or College of Valor. Gain its features at or below your current level for the rest of your career.

### Level 4, 8, 12, 16: Ability Score Improvement
Gain the Ability Score Improvement feat or another qualifying feat.

### Level 5: Font of Inspiration
Regain all expended Bardic Inspiration uses on a Short or Long Rest. Can also expend a spell slot (no action) to regain one use.

### Level 7: Countercharm
Reaction: if you or a creature within 30 ft fails a save against a Charmed- or Frightened-inflicting effect, cause the save to be rerolled with Advantage.

### Level 10: Magical Secrets
Whenever your Prepared Spells number increases, new prepared spells may be chosen from the Bard, Cleric, Druid, or Wizard spell lists (counting as Bard spells). Replacements can also draw from those lists thereafter.

### Level 18: Superior Inspiration
On rolling Initiative, regain expended Bardic Inspiration uses until you have at least two.

### Level 19: Epic Boon
Gain an Epic Boon feat or another qualifying feat. Boon of Spell Recall recommended.

### Level 20: Words of Creation
Always have *Power Word Heal* and *Power Word Kill* prepared. When cast, can target a second creature within 10 ft of the first.

## Bard Subclasses

### College of Dance
*Move in Harmony with the Cosmos.* These Bards believe the Words of Creation are uttered through the movement of celestial bodies and the smallest creatures, practicing agility, speed, and grace.

- **Level 3 — Dazzling Footwork** (while unarmored, no Shield):
  - **Dance Virtuoso:** Advantage on Charisma (Performance) checks involving dancing.
  - **Unarmored Defense:** base AC = 10 + Dex mod + Cha mod.
  - **Agile Strikes:** expending a Bardic Inspiration use as part of an action/Bonus Action/Reaction lets you also make an Unarmed Strike as part of it.
  - **Bardic Damage:** use Dexterity for Unarmed Strike attack rolls; damage can instead be a Bardic Inspiration die roll + Dex mod (Bludgeoning); this roll doesn't expend the die.
- **Level 6 — Inspiring Movement:** Reaction when a seen enemy ends its turn within 5 ft: expend a Bardic Inspiration use to move up to half Speed; one chosen ally within 30 ft can also move half Speed via its own Reaction. No Opportunity Attacks provoked.
- **Level 6 — Tandem Footwork:** on rolling Initiative (if not Incapacitated), expend a Bardic Inspiration use; roll the die — you and allies within 30 ft who can see/hear you gain that bonus to Initiative.
- **Level 14 — Leading Evasion:** on a Dex save that would halve damage, take none on success and only half on failure; can extend this to creatures within 5 ft making the same save. Unusable while Incapacitated.

### College of Glamour
*Weave Beguiling Fey Magic.* Rooted in Feywild magic, these Bards weave beauty and terror into performances, capable of cloaking themselves in otherworldly majesty.

- **Level 3 — Beguiling Magic:** always have *Charm Person* and *Mirror Image* prepared. After casting an Enchantment or Illusion spell with a slot, a seen creature within 60 ft makes a Wisdom save vs. your spell save DC or gains Charmed or Frightened (your choice) for 1 minute (repeats save each turn end). Once per Long Rest, or restore by expending a Bardic Inspiration use (no action).
- **Level 3 — Mantle of Inspiration:** Bonus Action, expend a Bardic Inspiration use and roll the die; choose creatures within 60 ft (up to Charisma modifier, min. 1) to gain Temporary HP = 2× the roll, and each can then use its Reaction to move up to its Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
- **Level 6 — Mantle of Majesty:** always have *Command* prepared. Bonus Action: cast *Command* free, gaining an unearthly appearance for 1 minute (or until Concentration ends), during which *Command* can be cast again as a Bonus Action without a slot. Creatures Charmed by you automatically fail their save against this *Command*. Once per Long Rest, or restore with a level 3+ spell slot (no action).
- **Level 14 — Unbreakable Majesty:** Bonus Action: assume a majestic presence for 1 minute or until Incapacitated. While active, the first hit against you each turn forces the attacker to succeed on a Charisma save (vs. your spell save DC) or the attack misses. Once per Short or Long Rest.

### College of Lore
*Plumb the Depths of Magical Knowledge.* These Bards collect spells and secrets from diverse sources, gathering in libraries and at public events to expose corruption and unravel lies.

- **Level 3 — Bonus Proficiencies:** proficiency with 3 skills of choice.
- **Level 3 — Cutting Words:** Reaction when a seen creature within 60 ft makes a damage roll or succeeds on an ability check/attack roll: expend a Bardic Inspiration use, roll the die, and subtract the result from that roll (potentially turning success into failure).
- **Level 6 — Magical Discoveries:** learn 2 spells from the Cleric, Druid, or Wizard lists (cantrip or spell-slot level); always prepared; replaceable on level-up with others meeting the same criteria.
- **Level 14 — Peerless Skill:** on failing an ability check or attack roll, expend a Bardic Inspiration use, roll the die, and add it to the d20 (potentially turning failure into success); the die isn't expended on a further failure.

### College of Valor
*Sing the Deeds of Ancient Heroes.* These Bards preserve the memory of great heroes through song, inspiring new generations toward similar accomplishment.

- **Level 3 — Combat Inspiration:** a creature holding your Bardic Inspiration die can use it for:
  - **Defense:** Reaction on being hit — roll the die, add to AC against that attack (potentially causing a miss).
  - **Offense:** immediately after hitting with an attack — roll the die, add to the damage.
- **Level 3 — Martial Training:** proficiency with Martial weapons; training with Medium armor and Shields. Can use a Simple or Martial weapon as a Spellcasting Focus for Bard spells.
- **Level 6 — Extra Attack:** attack twice instead of once on the Attack action. Can replace one of those attacks with a cantrip that has a casting time of an action.
- **Level 14 — Battle Magic:** after casting a spell with a casting time of an action, make one weapon attack as a Bonus Action.
