# Barbarian

*A Fierce Warrior of Primal Rage*

Barbarians channel primal forces of the multiverse into a Rage — not mere anger, but an incarnation of a predator's ferocity, a storm's fury, or a sea's turmoil. Some personify Rage as a spirit or forebear; others see it as a connection to the world's pain, wild magic, or their own deepest self. Rage fuels not just battle prowess but reflexes and senses. Barbarians often serve as protectors and leaders, charging headlong into danger for those they protect.

## Core Barbarian Traits

| Trait | Detail |
|---|---|
| Primary Ability | Strength |
| Hit Point Die | d12 per Barbarian level |
| Saving Throw Proficiencies | Strength and Constitution |
| Skill Proficiencies | Choose 2: Animal Handling, Athletics, Intimidation, Nature, Perception, or Survival |
| Weapon Proficiencies | Simple and Martial weapons |
| Armor Training | Light and Medium armor, and Shields |
| Starting Equipment | Choose A or B: (A) Greataxe, 4 Handaxes, Explorer's Pack, and 15 GP; or (B) 75 GP |

## Becoming a Barbarian

**As a Level 1 Character:** gain all Core Barbarian Traits, plus level 1 features from the Barbarian Features table.

**As a Multiclass Character:** gain Hit Point Die, Martial weapon proficiency, and Shield training from the Core Traits table, plus level 1 Barbarian features.

## Class Features

### Level 1: Rage
Bonus Action to enter Rage (if not wearing Heavy armor). Uses = Rages column; regain 1 use per Short Rest, all uses per Long Rest.

While active:
- **Damage Resistance:** Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.
- **Rage Damage:** bonus damage (per table) on Strength-based weapon/Unarmed Strike hits.
- **Strength Advantage:** Advantage on Strength checks and saving throws.
- **No Concentration or Spells:** can't maintain Concentration or cast spells.
- **Duration:** lasts until end of your next turn; ends early if you don Heavy armor or become Incapacitated. Extend another round by making an attack roll, forcing a save, or using a Bonus Action — up to 10 minutes total.

### Level 1: Unarmored Defense
Without armor, base AC = 10 + Dex mod + Con mod. Can still use a Shield.

### Level 1: Weapon Mastery
Use mastery properties of 2 kinds of Simple/Martial Melee weapons of choice; swap one choice after each Long Rest. Number of usable weapon types increases per the Weapon Mastery column.

### Level 2: Danger Sense
Advantage on Dexterity saving throws, unless Incapacitated.

### Level 2: Reckless Attack
On your first attack roll of the turn, attack recklessly: Advantage on Strength-based attack rolls until start of your next turn, but attack rolls against you also have Advantage during that time.

### Level 3: Barbarian Subclass
Choose a subclass: Path of the Berserker, Path of the Wild Heart, Path of the World Tree, or Path of the Zealot. Gain its features at or below your current level for the rest of your career.

### Level 3: Primal Knowledge
Gain proficiency in another skill from the Barbarian level 1 skill list. While Rage is active, can substitute a Strength check for an Acrobatics, Intimidation, Perception, Stealth, or Survival check.

### Level 4, 8, 12, 16: Ability Score Improvement
Gain the Ability Score Improvement feat or another qualifying feat.

### Level 5: Extra Attack
Attack twice instead of once when taking the Attack action.

### Level 5: Fast Movement
Speed +10 ft while not wearing Heavy armor.

### Level 7: Feral Instinct
Advantage on Initiative rolls.

### Level 7: Instinctive Pounce
As part of the Bonus Action to enter Rage, move up to half your Speed.

### Level 9: Brutal Strike
If using Reckless Attack, forgo Advantage on one chosen Strength-based attack roll (must not have Disadvantage). On a hit: extra 1d10 damage of the weapon/Unarmed Strike's type, plus one Brutal Strike effect:
- **Forceful Blow:** push target 15 ft straight away; you may move up to half Speed toward it without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
- **Hamstring Blow:** reduce target's Speed by 15 ft until start of your next turn (only the most recent Hamstring Blow applies).

### Level 11: Relentless Rage
If reduced to 0 HP while Raging (and not killed outright), make a DC 10 Constitution save; success changes HP to 2× Barbarian level instead. DC +5 each subsequent use since your last rest; resets to 10 after a Short or Long Rest.

### Level 13 & 17: Improved Brutal Strike
At 13, adds new Brutal Strike options:
- **Staggering Blow:** target has Disadvantage on its next save and can't make Opportunity Attacks until start of your next turn.
- **Sundering Blow:** the next attack roll by another creature against the target gains +5 (only one Sundering Blow bonus can apply at a time).

At 17: Brutal Strike extra damage increases to 2d10, and you can apply two different Brutal Strike effects per use.

### Level 15: Persistent Rage
Rolling Initiative regains all expended Rage uses (once per Long Rest). Rage now lasts a full 10 minutes without needing to extend it; ends early only on Unconscious condition or donning Heavy armor.

### Level 18: Indomitable Might
If a Strength check or save total is less than your Strength score, use the score instead.

### Level 19: Epic Boon
Gain an Epic Boon feat or another qualifying feat. Boon of Irresistible Offense recommended.

### Level 20: Primal Champion
Strength and Constitution scores each increase by 4 (max 25).

## Barbarian Subclasses

### Path of the Berserker
*Channel Rage into Violent Fury.* Berserkers direct their Rage toward pure violence, thriving in the chaos of battle.

- **Level 3 — Frenzy:** while Reckless Attack + Rage are active, deal extra damage (matching weapon/Unarmed Strike type) to the first target hit each turn — roll a number of d6s equal to your Rage Damage bonus.
- **Level 6 — Mindless Rage:** Immunity to Charmed and Frightened while Raging; entering Rage ends those conditions on you.
- **Level 10 — Retaliation:** Reaction to make one melee attack against a creature within 5 ft that damages you.
- **Level 14 — Intimidating Presence:** Bonus Action; each creature you choose in a 30-ft Emanation makes a Wisdom save (DC 8 + Str mod + Prof Bonus) or is Frightened for 1 minute (repeats save at end of each of its turns). Once per Long Rest, unless you spend a Rage use (no action) to restore it.

### Path of the Wild Heart
*Walk in Community with the Animal World.* These Barbarians see themselves as kin to animals, gaining magical communication with them and animal-themed Rage powers.

- **Level 3 — Animal Speaker:** cast *Beast Sense* and *Speak with Animals* as Rituals only (Wisdom-based).
- **Level 3 — Rage of the Wilds:** on activating Rage, choose one:
  - **Bear:** Resistance to all damage types except Force, Necrotic, Psychic, Radiant.
  - **Eagle:** the Rage bonus action can also include Disengage and Dash; while Raging, a Bonus Action can take both.
  - **Wolf:** allies have Advantage attacking any enemy within 5 ft of you.
- **Level 6 — Aspect of the Wilds:** choose one (changeable each Long Rest):
  - **Owl:** Darkvision 60 ft (or +60 ft if you already have it).
  - **Panther:** Climb Speed = Speed.
  - **Salmon:** Swim Speed = Speed.
- **Level 10 — Nature Speaker:** cast *Commune with Nature* as a Ritual only (Wisdom-based).
- **Level 14 — Power of the Wilds:** on activating Rage, choose one:
  - **Falcon:** Fly Speed = Speed while unarmored, during Rage.
  - **Lion:** enemies within 5 ft have Disadvantage attacking anyone but you or another Barbarian using this option.
  - **Ram:** melee hits can knock a Large or smaller creature Prone.

### Path of the World Tree
*Trace the Roots and Branches of the Multiverse.* These Barbarians connect through Rage to Yggdrasil, drawing on it for vitality and dimensional travel.

- **Level 3 — Vitality of the Tree:**
  - **Vitality Surge:** on activating Rage, gain Temporary HP = Barbarian level.
  - **Life-Giving Force:** at the start of each turn while Raging, choose another creature within 10 ft to gain Temporary HP (roll d6s equal to Rage Damage bonus). Unused Temp HP vanishes when Rage ends.
- **Level 6 — Branches of the Tree:** Reaction when a visible creature starts its turn within 30 ft while Raging: target makes a Strength save (DC 8 + Str mod + Prof Bonus) or teleports to an unoccupied space within 5 ft of you (or the nearest available); its Speed can then be reduced to 0 until end of the current turn.
- **Level 10 — Battering Roots:** reach +10 ft with Heavy or Versatile Melee weapons on your turn; a hit can add the Push or Topple mastery property alongside another mastery property in use.
- **Level 14 — Travel Along the Tree:** on activating Rage (and as a Bonus Action while Raging), teleport up to 60 ft to a seen unoccupied space. Once per Rage, extend range to 150 ft and bring up to 6 willing creatures within 10 ft of you, each teleporting to a space within 10 ft of your destination.

### Path of the Zealot
*Rage in Ecstatic Union with a God.* These Barbarians receive divine boons, experiencing Rage as spiritual union with a god or pantheon.

- **Level 3 — Divine Fury:** each turn while Raging, the first creature hit with a weapon/Unarmed Strike takes extra 1d6 + half Barbarian level (round down) damage, Necrotic or Radiant (your choice each time).
- **Level 3 — Warrior of the Gods:** pool of four d12s; Bonus Action to expend some, roll, and heal that total. Pool refills on Long Rest; max dice increases to 5 at level 6, 6 at level 12, 7 at level 17.
- **Level 6 — Fanatical Focus:** once per Rage, reroll a failed save with a bonus = Rage Damage bonus (must use new roll).
- **Level 10 — Zealous Presence:** Bonus Action; up to 10 chosen creatures within 60 ft gain Advantage on attack rolls and saving throws until start of your next turn. Once per Long Rest, unless you spend a Rage use (no action) to restore it.
- **Level 14 — Rage of the Gods:** on activating Rage, assume a divine warrior form for 1 minute or until reduced to 0 HP (once per Long Rest). While in this form:
  - **Flight:** Fly Speed = Speed, can hover.
  - **Resistance:** to Necrotic, Psychic, and Radiant damage.
  - **Revivification:** Reaction to expend a Rage use when a creature within 30 ft would drop to 0 HP, instead setting its HP to your Barbarian level.
