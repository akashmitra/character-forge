# Chapter 6: Equipment — Weapons

Equipment rules cover weapons, armor, and gear that characters buy or find. Availability and pricing at a given shop is up to the DM.

## Coins

Coins come in different denominations based on relative material value, priced relative to the Gold Piece (GP), the game's main currency. A coin weighs about 1/3 oz (50 coins = 1 lb).

| Coin | Value in GP |
|---|---|
| Copper Piece (CP) | 1/100 |
| Silver Piece (SP) | 1/10 |
| Electrum Piece (EP) | 1/2 |
| Gold Piece (GP) | 1 |
| Platinum Piece (PP) | 10 |

**Selling equipment:** sells for half its listed cost. Trade goods and valuables (gems, art objects) retain full value. Magic item prices are in the Dungeon Master's Guide.

## Weapons Overview

The Weapons table lists cost, weight, and for each weapon:

- **Category:** Simple or Martial (proficiencies are usually tied to category).
- **Melee or Ranged:** Melee attacks a target within 5 ft; Ranged attacks at greater distance.
- **Damage:** amount and type dealt on a hit.
- **Properties:** special rules (defined below).
- **Mastery:** a bonus property usable only if a character has a feature (e.g., Weapon Mastery) that unlocks it.

**Weapon Proficiency:** anyone can wield any weapon, but you only add your Proficiency Bonus to attack rolls with weapons you're proficient in. Monsters are proficient with any weapon in their stat block.

## Weapon Properties

- **Ammunition:** ranged attacks with this property require ammunition of the specified type; each attack uses one piece (a one-handed loading weapon needs a free hand). After a fight, spend 1 minute to recover half the ammunition used (round down); the rest is lost.
- **Finesse:** use Strength or Dexterity (your choice, same for both attack and damage rolls).
- **Heavy:** Disadvantage on attack rolls unless Strength ≥13 (melee) or Dexterity ≥13 (ranged).
- **Light:** taking the Attack action and attacking with a Light weapon lets you make one extra attack as a Bonus Action later that turn, using a different Light weapon; don't add your ability modifier to that extra attack's damage unless it's negative.
- **Loading:** only one piece of ammunition can be fired per action/Bonus Action/Reaction, regardless of extra attacks.
- **Range:** listed as (normal/long) in feet. Attacks beyond normal range have Disadvantage; you can't attack beyond long range.
- **Reach:** adds 5 feet to your reach (including for Opportunity Attacks).
- **Thrown:** the weapon can be thrown for a ranged attack, drawing it as part of the attack; if it's a Melee weapon, use the same ability modifier as its melee attack.
- **Two-Handed:** requires two hands to attack with.
- **Versatile:** can be used one- or two-handed; the parenthetical damage applies when used two-handed.

*Improvised weapons (using an object as a makeshift weapon, or using a weapon in an unusual way) are covered under "Improvised Weapons" in the rules glossary.*

## Mastery Properties

Usable only with a feature (e.g., Weapon Mastery) that unlocks them.

- **Cleave:** on a melee hit, make an additional melee attack roll against a second creature within 5 ft of the first and within your reach; on a hit, deal weapon damage (no ability modifier unless negative). Once per turn.
- **Graze:** if your attack misses, deal damage equal to the ability modifier used for the attack (same damage type as the weapon); increases only by increasing that ability modifier.
- **Nick:** the Light property's extra attack can be made as part of the Attack action instead of a Bonus Action. Once per turn.
- **Push:** on a hit, push the creature up to 10 ft straight away if it's Large or smaller.
- **Sap:** on a hit, the target has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of your next turn.
- **Slow:** on a hit that deals damage, reduce the target's Speed by 10 ft until the start of your next turn (multiple hits don't stack beyond −10 ft).
- **Topple:** on a hit, force a Constitution save (DC 8 + ability modifier used + Proficiency Bonus); failure = Prone.
- **Vex:** on a hit that deals damage, gain Advantage on your next attack roll against that creature before the end of your next turn.
